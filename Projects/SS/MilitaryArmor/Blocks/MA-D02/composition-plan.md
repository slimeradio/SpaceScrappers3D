# MA-D02 composition plan

Catalog rotary-drill-2x3: visual-only mechanical drill. Reuse MA-D01-P02 v1.0 drive and MA-D01-P03 v1.0 cutter twice each at unit scale. Single MA-D01-P01 frame is incompatible with two spindles; create MA-D02-P01 twin-spindle cradle with two bearing seats, broad aft crossmember, outer load rails and protected central spine. No duplication of compatible drives/cutters. Two head centers at X +/-1 BU have clearance. BU is not metres. Static authoring, +Z up, -Y tool forward. Gate new source before assembly; no edits to original D01 parts.
